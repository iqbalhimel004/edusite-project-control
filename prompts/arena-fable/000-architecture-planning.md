# Arena Fable Prompt 000 — Architecture Planning

## Purpose
Give Fable the complete product intent first and require planning only before any implementation.

## Core instruction used
Fable was instructed to act as senior UI/UX architect and frontend engineering specialist for a reusable commercial Bangladeshi high-school website platform; not to build a backend; to plan Next.js/TypeScript/Tailwind architecture, white-label configuration, domain/data-provider separation, responsive UX, accessibility, future Frappe compatibility, route structure, design-system foundations and staged implementation; and to wait for approval before coding.

## Important constraints
- frontend only at this stage;
- future Frappe/ERPNext/Frappe Education integration;
- reusable white-label product rather than one-off landing page;
- premium academic visual identity;
- realistic Bangladesh-school information architecture;
- small controlled stages, not one-shot generation;
- do not silently redesign approved work;
- do not implement backend/auth/payment/database;
- report conflicts before changing accepted architecture.

The full architectural response and Technical Lead amendments are summarized in `reviews/arena-stage0-architecture.md`; canonical decisions are represented in ADR/spec files rather than relying on this prompt text.
